How to play

The app plays Sir. Each round Sir draws cards and the total is the Punishment. Boy holds a hand of cards and can counter with Mercy. Here is a sample round:

Face cards and Jokers either remove 1 clothing or add 1 restraint, depending on this switch. Max means nothing is left to remove or add, so only swats count.

New Game+

When the Drawing Pile runs out, start New Game+: the discards are reshuffled, Sir draws 1 more card each round, and Boy keeps his hand.

Two players

On a landscape tablet or desktop you can play with two Boys. Each Boy has his own cards, hand, and Punishment; a random Boy gets priority each round, but either may act. A Boy can Counter for himself or Transfer his Mercy to the other Boy. A transferred Straight gives the other Boy your Privilege, and with a transferred Flush you pick his implement. Each Boy has his own color.
